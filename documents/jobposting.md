## Why This Market

Self-custody is winning. Hardware wallet sales keep climbing. Multisig is going mainstream. After FTX, "not your keys, not your coins" isn't fringe anymore.

Existing tax tools (Koinly, CoinTracker, etc.) are built for exchange traders. They handle self-custody as an afterthought. No real multisig support. Bad xpub handling. Internal transfers create fake taxable events.

We're building for the people those tools ignore.
